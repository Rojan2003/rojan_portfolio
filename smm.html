Case study

Lead capture and qualification system

  1. Form
  2. Opportunity created
  3. Tag + team notification
  4. Qualified?
    • YesQualified stage
    • NoNeeds review
  • Built in GoHighLevel for a client selling lead generation to commercial HVAC companies.
  • An inquiry form with 13 fields feeds a seven-stage sales pipeline.
  • A workflow creates the opportunity, tags the contact and notifies the team.
  • An if/else step moves qualified leads forward automatically and flags the rest for a person to review.
  • Tested with two sample leads through both paths, with every step confirmed in the execution logs.
  • Delivered with a step-by-step build guide.
  • Built and tested in one evening.

Automations I can set up for your social media

  • Comment-to-DM

    Someone comments a keyword on a post and instantly gets a DM with a link or guide (ManyChat).

  • Instant replies

    Automatic first answers to common questions like pricing, hours and shipping, with a person taking over after.

  • Lead capture from DMs

    Collecting a name and email in the chat and sending the lead into a CRM pipeline.

  • Follow-up sequences

    Emails or messages that go out on a schedule after someone inquires.

  • Team alerts

    A notification the moment a serious lead comes in.

  • Weekly summary

    A simple report of leads, replies and follow-ups due.
