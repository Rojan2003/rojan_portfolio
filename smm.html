Practice build

Content request to caption draft (n8n)

A practice build on my own n8n setup, not a client project.

  1. A form collects the topic, platform, tone and post date.
  2. The details are prepared and the hashtags are built.
  3. LinkedIn gets one caption layout, Instagram/Facebook gets another.
  4. The draft is shown with the status "Needs approval", so nothing is posted without sign-off.

Captions are template-based for now, and an AI step can be added later.

Automations I can set up for your social media

  • Comment-to-DM

    Someone comments a keyword on a post and instantly gets a DM with a link or guide (ManyChat).

  • Instant replies

    Automatic first answers to common questions like pricing, hours and shipping, with a person taking over after.

  • Lead capture from DMs

    Collecting a name and email in the chat and sending the lead into a CRM pipeline.

  • Follow-up sequences

    Emails or messages that go out on a schedule after someone inquires.

  • Team alerts

    A notification the moment a serious lead comes in.

  • Weekly summary

    A simple report of leads, replies and follow-ups due.

Tools: GoHighLevel, n8n, ManyChat, Google Apps Script, Claude and Claude Code, REST APIs and webhooks
